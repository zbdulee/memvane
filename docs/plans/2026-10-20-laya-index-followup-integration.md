The six serial groups and the three Node-discovered helper files all passed and settled on the integrated branch: 1,648 tests, 1,639 PASS, 9 SKIP, 0 FAIL. The parent clarified the established policy in [worktree search/sync policy](../git-worktree-search-sync-policy.md): a returned candidate must be checked against the current file and may not silently disappear into a shorter or empty answer; an unreturned new file may remain unsearchable until async indexing catches up. The whole-corpus proof was therefore removed from the product path and the previous commit inverted in the working tree. Selected capacity recall now fails the complete answer when a **returned** candidate changed or cannot be read, asks for sync on a confirmed returned-row mismatch, and reports `source_unavailable` on a returned read/budget refusal. Exclusions remain filters. An unreturned new file is index lag and does not force a corpus walk. The candidate per-file read bound now matches the provider's 5 MB document bound.
