정상 경로: `(absent)` `reserve`→`reserved` (`general_gold_ledger.reserve`) → `before_launch`→`dispatch_unknown` → `after_launch`→`dispatched` → `answer`→`answer_received` → `commit`→`committed` (모두 `default_teacher._call`).
보류: `answer_received` `park`→`answer_parked` (`default_teacher._park`); `answer_parked` `confirm`→`committed` (`default_teacher._confirm_parked`), `reject_kept`→`dispatched_uncertain` (`default_teacher._review_parked`).
