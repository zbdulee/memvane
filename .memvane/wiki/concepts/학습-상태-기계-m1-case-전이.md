## Summary
`docs/dev/learning-state.md` M1 case. 저장 위치는 learning DB `cases.status`, lock은 `project-worker.lock`이다. 쓰는 주체는 hook capture child(insert), teacher pass, rotation and migration(delete)이다. 상태는 `(absent)`, `queued`, `first_running`, `first_done`, `gold`(종결), `pending_review`(종결)이다.
전이:
- `(absent)` `capture`→`queued` (`shared.enqueue`)
- `queued`: `claim`→`first_running`, `local_check_failed`→`pending_review` (`default_teacher._default_case`)
- `first_running`: `answer_committed`→`gold` (`default_teacher._call`), `answer_kept`→`first_done` (`default_teacher._park`), `content_refused`·`not_sent_exhausted`·`uncertain`→`pending_review` 및 `not_sent`·`provider_refused`→`queued` (`default_teacher._failed`), `pass_start`→`pending_review` (`default_teacher.process_pending_default`)
- `first_done`: `consent_withdrawn`→`pending_review` (`default_teacher._review_parked`), `kept_confirmed`→`gold` 및 `kept_rejected`→`pending_review` (`default_teacher._confirm_parked`)
- 모든 비-absent 상태: `git_migrated`→`(absent)` (`edit_git_migration.migrate`), `prompt_retired`→`(absent)` (`prompt_retirement.retire`)
- `rotation_historical`→`(absent)` (`case_rotation.rotate`): `queued`·`gold`·`pending_review`; `rotation`→`(absent)`: `gold`·`pending_review`
